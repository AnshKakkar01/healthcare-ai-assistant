MEDICATION REFILL POLICY
Document Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: February 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the procedures and criteria for processing medication
refill requests to ensure patient safety, regulatory compliance, and efficient
pharmacy operations.

2. GENERAL REFILL ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
A prescription may be refilled if all of the following conditions are met:
- The original prescription was issued by an authorized provider at this facility.
- The medication is not expired or discontinued.
- The patient has not exceeded the authorized number of refills.
- There are no outstanding lab results or monitoring requirements that must be
  reviewed before the next refill.
- The patient has not been flagged for medication non-adherence or misuse.

3. REFILL REQUEST CHANNELS
Patients may request refills through the following channels:
a) Patient Portal: Log in to the patient portal and submit a refill request under
   "Medications." Allow 48–72 business hours for processing.
b) Mobile App: Use the "Refill Rx" feature. Same processing time applies.
c) Pharmacy Direct: Some participating pharmacies can electronically request refills
   on the patient's behalf with prior authorization.
d) Telehealth Visit: A refill may be reviewed and approved during a scheduled
   telehealth appointment (see Telehealth Guidelines, Section 4).
e) In-Person Visit: Patients may request refills during any scheduled appointment.
f) Phone: Call the pharmacy line at 1-800-MED-REFILL. Not recommended for
   controlled substances.

4. PROCESSING TIMEFRAMES
- Non-urgent refills: 48–72 business hours
- Urgent refills (less than 3-day supply remaining): Same business day if requested
  before 12 PM; otherwise next business day.
- After-hours urgent refills: A limited emergency supply of up to 72 hours may be
  dispensed at the pharmacist's discretion.
- Controlled substances: Up to 5 business days due to additional verification steps.

5. CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE REFILL POLICY
- Schedule II medications (e.g., Adderall, OxyContin) cannot be refilled; a new
  prescription is required each time.
- Schedule III–IV medications may be refilled up to 5 times within 6 months of
  the original prescription date.
- All controlled substance requests require an in-person visit or, in limited
  cases, a video telehealth visit with identity verification.
- Random urine drug screening may be required as part of controlled substance
  management.

6. MEDICATIONS REQUIRING MONITORING BEFORE REFILL
The following medication classes require recent lab work before a refill is authorized:
- Warfarin / Coumadin: INR must be on file within the past 30 days.
- Metformin: Kidney function (eGFR) must be on file within the past 90 days.
- Lithium: Serum lithium levels must be on file within the past 60 days.
- ACE Inhibitors: Potassium and creatinine levels within 90 days.
- Thyroid medications (Levothyroxine): TSH within the past 6 months.
If lab work is not on file, the refill request will be placed on hold and the patient
will be notified via the portal or phone.

7. DENIALS AND APPEALS
Refill requests may be denied for the following reasons:
- Prescription has expired or reached maximum refill limit.
- Outstanding required lab work not completed.
- Insurance prior authorization not obtained.
- Provider has placed a clinical hold on the medication.
Patients may appeal a denial by contacting the pharmacy at 1-800-MED-REFILL and
requesting a clinical review. Appeals are typically resolved within 2 business days.

8. AUTOMATIC REFILL PROGRAM
- Patients may enroll in the Auto-Refill Program for eligible maintenance medications.
- Enrollment is available via the patient portal under "Prescription Management."
- Auto-refill is not available for controlled substances or medications requiring
  prior monitoring.
- Patients will be notified by email/SMS 7 days before each auto-refill is processed.

9. INSURANCE AND PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
- Certain medications require insurance prior authorization (PA) before being dispensed.
- PA requests are submitted by the provider's office on behalf of the patient.
- PA processing time: 2–5 business days depending on the insurer.
- If PA is denied, the provider will discuss alternative medications with the patient.
