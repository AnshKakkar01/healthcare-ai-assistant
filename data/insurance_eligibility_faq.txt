INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY AND BENEFITS — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Document Version: 1.5 | Effective Date: February 1, 2024

Q1: How do I know if my insurance is accepted at your facility?
A: You can verify insurance acceptance by:
- Visiting our website at www.healthcarefacility.org/insurance
- Calling our Insurance Verification Desk at 1-800-INS-VERIFY (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM)
- Logging into the patient portal and entering your insurance details
We currently accept most major commercial plans including BlueCross BlueShield,
Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana, and Molina Healthcare.

Q2: What insurance information do I need to provide?
A: Please bring the following to every visit:
- Insurance card (front and back)
- Government-issued photo ID
- Secondary insurance card (if applicable)
- Referral authorization number (if required by your plan)

Q3: What is a copay and when do I pay it?
A: A copay is a fixed amount you pay at the time of service. Copay amounts vary
by plan and service type (e.g., primary care visit vs. specialist visit). Copays are
due at check-in before your appointment begins. We accept cash, card, and payment
via the patient portal.

Q4: What is a deductible?
A: A deductible is the amount you must pay out-of-pocket each year before your
insurance begins to cover costs. For example, if your deductible is $1,500, you
pay the first $1,500 of covered services each year before insurance kicks in.
Copays and premiums typically do not count toward your deductible.

Q5: What is coinsurance?
A: After meeting your deductible, coinsurance is the percentage of costs you
share with your insurer. For example, with 20% coinsurance, your insurer pays
80% of the allowed amount and you pay 20%.

Q6: What is an out-of-pocket maximum?
A: This is the most you will have to pay for covered services in a plan year.
Once reached, your insurance covers 100% of covered in-network services.
Check your insurance card or member portal for your specific limit.

Q7: What is prior authorization (PA) and do I need one?
A: Prior authorization is advance approval from your insurance company before
receiving certain services or medications. PA is commonly required for:
- Specialist referrals (some plans)
- Elective surgeries
- Certain imaging (MRI, CT scans)
- High-cost or brand-name medications
Our billing team will handle PA requests on your behalf. Proceeding without
required PA may result in denial of coverage.

Q8: How do I check my eligibility and benefits before a visit?
A: Call the member services number on the back of your insurance card, or
log into your insurer's member portal. You can also contact our Insurance
Verification Desk at 1-800-INS-VERIFY and we will verify on your behalf.

Q9: What happens if my insurance is out-of-network?
A: Out-of-network services may be partially covered or not covered at all,
depending on your plan. You may be responsible for the full billed amount.
We recommend confirming network status before every visit. Our billing team
can provide an estimate of costs for out-of-network services.

Q10: What if I don't have insurance?
A: Uninsured patients are eligible for our Sliding Fee Discount Program, which
adjusts costs based on household income and family size. To apply:
- Complete the financial assistance application at any registration desk.
- Submit proof of income (pay stubs, tax return, or income affidavit).
- Applications are reviewed within 5 business days.
We also accept payment plans and can connect you with enrollment assistance
for Medicaid, marketplace plans, or other state programs.

Q11: I received a bill after my visit. What does it mean?
A: After your visit, your insurance processes the claim and sends an Explanation
of Benefits (EOB). The bill you receive reflects your patient responsibility after
insurance payment. Review your EOB and bill together to verify accuracy. For
billing questions, contact: 1-800-BILL-HELP or billing@healthcarefacility.org.

Q12: What is Medicare and Medicaid?
A: Medicare is a federal insurance program for people 65+ and those with
certain disabilities. Medicaid is a joint federal-state program for low-income
individuals and families. We accept both Medicare and Medicaid. Coverage details
vary; contact Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE or Medicaid at your state office.

Q13: Are lab tests and imaging covered?
A: Coverage depends on your plan. Preventive lab work ordered as part of an
annual wellness exam is often covered at 100% in-network. Diagnostic tests
ordered for specific symptoms are subject to your deductible and coinsurance.
Always confirm coverage before non-urgent lab or imaging orders.

Q14: What if a claim is denied?
A: If a claim is denied, you and your provider will receive a denial notice.
You have the right to appeal. Our billing team can help file the appeal.
Common denial reasons include: missing PA, incorrect coding, or services deemed
not medically necessary. Appeal deadlines vary by insurer but are typically
30–180 days from the denial date.
