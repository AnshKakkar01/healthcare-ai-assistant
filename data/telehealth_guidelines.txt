TELEHEALTH CONSULTATION GUIDELINES
Document Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
Telehealth services allow patients to receive medical consultations remotely via
video, phone, or secure messaging. These guidelines outline the scope, eligibility,
and procedures for telehealth consultations at our facility.

2. ELIGIBLE CONDITIONS FOR TELEHEALTH
The following conditions and concerns are appropriate for telehealth visits:
- Minor illnesses: cold, flu, sore throat, urinary tract infections
- Follow-up consultations for chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, asthma)
- Mental health and behavioral health counseling
- Prescription refill reviews (non-controlled medications)
- Dermatology consultations with photo submission
- Nutrition and lifestyle counseling
- Post-surgical follow-ups (non-wound-related)
- Lab result reviews and discussions

3. CONDITIONS REQUIRING IN-PERSON VISITS
The following require an in-person evaluation and cannot be handled via telehealth:
- Chest pain or suspected cardiac emergency
- Difficulty breathing or respiratory distress
- Suspected fractures or physical trauma
- Conditions requiring physical examination (hernia, lumps, etc.)
- First-time prescriptions for controlled substances
- Annual physical exams and preventive screenings

4. MEDICATION REFILLS THROUGH TELEHEALTH
- Medication refill requests may be reviewed during telehealth visits.
- Refills are permitted only for medications that are already prescribed and
  do not require an in-person evaluation or physical monitoring.
- Controlled substances (Schedule II-IV) cannot be prescribed via telehealth
  under standard protocols.
- The provider must review the patient's current medication list, last refill date,
  and any reported side effects before approving the refill.
- Maximum refill period via telehealth is 90 days.

5. SCHEDULING A TELEHEALTH APPOINTMENT
- Patients may schedule telehealth appointments via the patient portal, mobile app,
  or by calling the scheduling hotline at 1-800-HEALTH-1.
- Appointments are available Monday through Saturday, 7 AM to 9 PM.
- Same-day telehealth slots may be available for urgent non-emergency concerns.
- A minimum of 15 minutes should be booked for any telehealth consultation.

6. TECHNOLOGY REQUIREMENTS
- Patients need a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone.
- A stable internet connection with at least 5 Mbps upload/download speed is required.
- Supported platforms: Zoom for Healthcare, Doxy.me, or the facility's patient portal app.
- Patients should test their setup 10 minutes before the appointment.

7. INSURANCE AND BILLING
- Most major insurance plans cover telehealth services at the same copay as in-person visits.
- Medicare and Medicaid telehealth coverage follows current CMS guidelines.
- Patients are advised to verify telehealth coverage with their insurance prior to the visit.
- Out-of-pocket cost for uninsured patients: $49 per standard telehealth visit.

8. PRIVACY AND SECURITY
- All telehealth sessions are conducted on HIPAA-compliant platforms.
- Sessions are not recorded unless explicit written consent is obtained.
- Patients should conduct telehealth visits from a private location.
- Providers are not permitted to conduct telehealth sessions from public spaces.

9. EMERGENCY PROTOCOL
- If a patient presents with a life-threatening emergency during a telehealth session,
  the provider will instruct the patient to call 911 immediately.
- The provider will stay on the call until emergency services are confirmed.
