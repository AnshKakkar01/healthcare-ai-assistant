APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING POLICY
Document Version: 1.8 | Effective Date: January 15, 2024

1. PURPOSE
This policy outlines the procedures for scheduling, rescheduling, and canceling
patient appointments across all departments at our facility.

2. SCHEDULING CHANNELS
Patients may book appointments through:
a) Online Patient Portal: portal.healthcarefacility.org (24/7 availability)
b) Mobile App: "MyHealth" app (iOS and Android)
c) Phone: 1-800-APPT-NOW (Monday–Friday, 7 AM–7 PM; Saturday, 8 AM–2 PM)
d) In-Person: Front desk at any of our clinic locations
e) Telehealth: Available via the portal or app for eligible conditions

3. APPOINTMENT TYPES AND LEAD TIMES
- New Patient Appointment: Book at least 5–7 business days in advance.
- Follow-Up Appointment: Typically available within 2–5 business days.
- Annual Physical / Wellness Visit: Schedule at least 2–4 weeks in advance.
- Specialist Referral: Requires a referral from your primary care provider; lead time
  varies by specialty (1–4 weeks typical).
- Urgent Same-Day Appointment: Limited slots available; call by 9 AM for same-day.
- Telehealth Appointments: Available with shorter lead times; often same-day.

4. REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR BOOKING
When scheduling, please have the following ready:
- Full legal name and date of birth
- Insurance card or member ID
- Reason for visit / symptoms (a brief description)
- Referring provider name (if applicable)
- Preferred date, time, and location
- Contact phone number and email address

5. APPOINTMENT REMINDERS
- Automated reminders are sent 72 hours and 24 hours before the appointment.
- Reminders are sent via email, SMS, or phone call based on the patient's preference.
- Patients can confirm, reschedule, or cancel via the reminder link.

6. CANCELLATION AND RESCHEDULING POLICY
- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment.
- Late cancellations (under 24 hours): A $25 late cancellation fee may be applied.
- No-shows (failure to appear without notice): A $40 no-show fee may be charged.
- Fees may be waived in cases of documented medical emergencies.
- To cancel or reschedule: use the patient portal, app, or call 1-800-APPT-NOW.

7. WALK-IN APPOINTMENTS
- Walk-in visits are accepted at designated Urgent Care locations only.
- Walk-in availability is subject to provider capacity on the day of visit.
- Wait times for walk-ins vary; estimated wait time is displayed at the front desk.
- Walk-ins are not available for specialist consultations.

8. NEW PATIENT REGISTRATION
New patients must complete the following before their first appointment:
- Online registration form (sent via email link after booking)
- Medical history questionnaire
- Insurance verification
- HIPAA consent form
- Signed patient rights and responsibilities form
All forms should be completed at least 48 hours before the appointment.

9. REFERRALS AND SPECIALIST APPOINTMENTS
- Primary Care Provider (PCP) referrals are required by most insurance plans
  before seeing a specialist.
- Referrals are valid for 90 days from the issue date unless otherwise noted.
- The PCP office will submit the referral electronically; the patient will be
  notified by email/SMS once it is approved.
- Patients should not book a specialist appointment before the referral is approved.

10. DEPARTMENTS AND AVAILABLE SPECIALTIES
Available departments for scheduling:
- Cardiology (Heart Health)
- Orthopedics (Bone and Joint)
- Neurology (Brain and Nervous System)
- Endocrinology (Hormones and Diabetes)
- Dermatology (Skin)
- Gastroenterology (Digestive System)
- Oncology (Cancer Care)
- Pediatrics (Children's Health, ages 0–17)
- OB/GYN (Women's Health)
- Psychiatry and Behavioral Health
- General Surgery
- Primary Care / Family Medicine

11. APPOINTMENT NO-SHOW MANAGEMENT
- Patients with 3 or more no-shows within a 12-month period may be required to
  prepay for future appointments.
- Patients with 5 or more no-shows may be placed on a waiting-list-only scheduling
  tier with limited advance booking options.
